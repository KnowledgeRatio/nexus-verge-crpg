🗺️ Exploration

World Generation: Each game seed creates a unique procedural world with 18 terrain types, settlements, dungeons, and points of interest.

Fog of War: Unexplored areas are hidden. Explored tiles remain visible but dimmed. Your vision radius reveals nearby areas.

Random Encounters: Moving through terrain has a chance to trigger combat encounters. Terrain difficulty affects encounter rate (1% base, scaled by terrain).

Settlements: Villages, towns, and cities offer NPCs, quests, trading, and rest opportunities. Press E when adjacent to enter.

⚔️ Combat

Initiative: Turn order determined by d20 + DEX modifier. Combat is turn-based with action economy.

Actions: Each turn you have an Action, Bonus Action, and Reaction (not yet implemented).

Attacking: Roll d20 + ability modifier + proficiency bonus vs enemy AC. Natural 20 = critical hit (double damage dice).

Weapon Masteries: Special effects based on equipped weapon (Cleave, Graze, Nick, Push, Sap, Slow, Topple, Vex).

Fleeing: Roll d20 + initiative bonus vs DC 30. Success returns you to exploration.

📈 Character Progression

Experience: Gain XP from combat victories based on enemy CR. Level up when reaching XP thresholds (follows D&D 5e progression).

HP & Hit Dice: HP = class hit die + CON modifier per level. Hit dice used during short rests for healing.

Abilities: Six ability scores (STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA) determine modifiers for all rolls and stats.

Skills: 13 skills based on abilities. Proficiency bonus applies to skills you're proficient in.

💤 Rest System

Short Rest: Roll all hit dice for healing. Maximum 2 per long rest. Can be taken anywhere safe.

Long Rest: Fully restore HP, recover half hit dice, restore spell slots, reset short rest counter. Requires tavern/inn or sanctuary.

Sanctuaries: Safe rest locations (☼) scattered across wilderness. Allow long rests without settlements.

💰 Trading & Economy

Merchants: Found in settlements. Sell weapons, armor, consumables, and equipment.

Charisma Pricing: Your CHA modifier affects prices (±1% per modifier point). Higher CHA = better deals.

Inventory: Carrying capacity = STR × 15 lbs. Equip items from inventory to use them.

Equipment Slots: Main Hand, Off-Hand, Armor, accessories. Light weapons can dual-wield.

📜 Quests

Quest Givers: NPCs in settlements offer quests based on their roles (leaders, merchants, innkeepers, guards).

Quest Types: Kill, Retrieve, Deliver, Explore, Skill Challenges. Objectives tracked automatically.

Rewards: XP, gold, items, reputation. Campaign quests advance the main storyline.

Quest Log: Press Q to view active, completed, and failed quests. Track specific quests to highlight them.

🎲 D&D 5e Rules

Proficiency Bonus: Starts at +2, increases with level. Applies to proficient skills, saves, and attacks.

Armor Class (AC): Base 10 + DEX modifier + armor bonus + shield bonus.

Ability Modifiers: (Ability Score - 10) ÷ 2, rounded down. Applied to relevant rolls.

Advantage/Disadvantage: Roll 2d20, take higher (advantage) or lower (disadvantage).

Critical Hits: Natural 20 on attack roll = double damage dice (not modifiers).

💡 Tips & Tricks

  • Save frequently using ESC menu. 5 save slots available.
  • Rest at settlements before long journeys. Sanctuaries are rare but valuable.
  • Match weapon masteries to your playstyle. Nick for dual-wielding, Cleave for AoE, etc.
  • Higher ground and cover don't exist in this simplified combat, focus on equipment and positioning order.
  • Check character sheet (C) to review your stats, features, and equipment.
  • World seeds are shareable! Same seed = same world layout and settlements.
  • Terrain affects encounter rates: Roads are safer, mountains and swamps are dangerous.
