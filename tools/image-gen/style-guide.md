# Nexus Verge — Image Generation Style Guide

This file is maintained by the worldbuilder. The image generator reads it verbatim and injects
each section into the prompt for the matching asset type. Keep each section tight — shorter
instructions produce more consistent results.

---

## Art Style

Painterly dark-fantasy illustration. Desaturated earth tones — ash grey, iron brown, deep slate —
with isolated warm pops of amber or ember light. Ink-defined silhouettes, rough brushwork
textures, visible grain. Mood: weathered and weary, not grimdark. Upper-left light source,
consistent across all assets. Square format, clean edges, no borders or frames. No photo-realism;
no anime; no bright saturated fantasy.

## World Setting

Nexus Verge is a world broken by a catastrophe called the Fracture — an ancient magical event
that shattered stable terrain and left wild, unstable zones between settlements. Civilisation
is rough and functional: stone walls patched with salvaged timber, markets selling salvaged goods,
guards who look tired. Magic is rare and makes people uneasy. Nature has warped — forests grow
wrong, animals have changed. Three callings define how people survive: Dedication (force of arms),
Scholar (cautious arcane study), Wanderlust (movement and adaptation).

## Terrain Tiles

Top-down orthographic, 64×64 pixel density. Readable at thumbnail scale. Earthy, desaturated
palette — each biome gets one identifying accent: marshes get sickly yellow-green, forests get
dark teal shadow, mountains get blue-grey frost, plains get pale straw. Textures suggest history:
cracked stone, overgrown paths, scorched ground. No characters or creatures. Seamless edges.
Pixel-art adjacent — clean, not painterly at tile scale.

## Monster Art

Three-quarter view, creature fills 70% of frame. Dramatic but grounded pose — threat-display,
not heroic. Humanoids look desperate and hungry, not monstrous-for-fun. Undead are grey and
desiccated, cold blue shadows. Beasts are naturalistic but wrong — slightly too large, too many
teeth, wrong proportions. Background: dark atmospheric environment, blurred, no competing detail.
No UI chrome. Lighting from upper-left, single source.

## Item Icons

Isometric angle, centred on near-black background with soft vignette. Single item only, no
hands. Materials rendered with clear texture: leather shows cracked grain, iron shows hammer
marks, wood shows split grain. Magic items have a restrained inner glow — unsettling, not
triumphant. Rarity by colour temperature: common = raw iron and worn leather, uncommon = faint
blue-silver sheen, rare = cold violet edge-light, legendary = deep amber aura with visible heat
distortion.

## Character Portraits

Bust portrait, subject centred, face occupying top half. Expression: serious, watchful — not
smiling. Lighting: single warm source from upper-left, cool fill from opposite side. Background:
soft-blurred ruin or settlement appropriate to race — Humans in stone towns, Elves in overgrown
remnants, Dwarves in carved rock interiors, Halflings in low timber buildings, Dragonborn in
ash-scorched open terrain. Costume is functional and worn. No ornate decoration unless the
character is nobility. Slightly desaturated background keeps focus on the face.

## Negative Prompts

photorealistic photography, 3D render, CGI, watermark, signature, text, UI elements, cartoon,
chibi, anime, bright saturated fantasy colours, white or light background, floating in void,
cropped limbs, extra fingers, duplicate subjects, modern clothing or technology, clean or
pristine equipment, smiling expressions, ornate decorative borders, lens flare, bloom effects
