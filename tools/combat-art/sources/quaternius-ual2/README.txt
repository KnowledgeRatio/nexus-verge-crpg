The female mannequin doesn’t include the animations, as duplicating them wouldn’t make sense. 
You can easily retarget them from the library files in Blender, or even better, directly in your engine, since both mannequins share the same rig and very similar proportions.The Universal Animation Library comes in two files: the one ending in _RM has root motion baked into every animation, while the other has root motion disabled.

Explore all the animations in the Animation Viewer!
