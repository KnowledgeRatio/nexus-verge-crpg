# Creative Director Agent Memory

## North Star

- Tone: **Desperate wonder. Survival on a living frontier.**
- Joy comes from agency, discovery, mastery, expressive builds, earned relief, memorable surprise, and attachment under pressure.
- The convergence premise should appear through specific incompatible materials, ecologies, cultures, and technologies—not generic multiverse spectacle.
- The Void is indifferent physics, never emotional evil.
- Prefer one clear emotional thesis over a pile of individually attractive elements.

## Decision Rights

- User: creative sponsor and final decision-maker.
- Creative Director: cross-discipline experiential target, aesthetic grammar, pacing, and coherence.
- Product Owner: roadmap, scope, sequencing, and product acceptance.
- Game Designer: mechanics and balance intent.
- Worldbuilder: canon and player-facing narrative.
- Frontend Developer: interaction and presentation implementation.
- Creative Prompt Engineer: model-facing prompt execution.

## Visual Baseline

- Illustrative oil-painting concept-art aesthetic with visible impasto, strong value contrast, earth-tone foundation, and restrained focal saturation.
- Worn, repaired, improvised materials; multiple-origin details and rift-geometry seams.
- Avoid generic bright fantasy, photorealism, anime, and indiscriminate modern/industrial saturation.
