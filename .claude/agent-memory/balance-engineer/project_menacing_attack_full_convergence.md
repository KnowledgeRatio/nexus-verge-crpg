Follow-up to [[project-menacing-attack-presence-kicker]]. `game-designer` rejected the asymmetric Presence "kicker" and requested genuine convergence instead: `DC = 8 + proficiency + floor(Prowess_mod/2) + floor(Presence_mod/2)`, replacing today's live `8 + prof + Prowess_mod` entirely (Trip/Pushing/Disarming Attack keep the unchanged full-weight formula — `EffectDispatcher.js:427-432 maneuverSaveDC`, shared by all four Exemplar maneuvers today, now diverges for Menacing Attack only).

**Q1 — DC delta vs today's baseline, L3-L10, real point-buy + every-level PC ASI ([[reference-asi-every-level-not-asilevels-gated]]):** PureProwess (Presence dumped to point-buy floor 8, mod -1, never invested) loses **-3 DC at L3/L5, -4 DC at L7/L10** vs today. Balanced (alternates ASI Prowess/Presence) loses only **-1 at L3/L5/L10, +0 at L7** — essentially DC-neutral. This is a much bigger tax on the pure-Prowess archetype than the rejected kicker ever produced (kicker's max real-world swing was ±1-2 DC; true 50/50 convergence structurally costs a dump-build 2-4x more, because it now loses half its OWN stat's contribution too, not just missing out on a bonus term). This is the expected, correct behavior of genuine convergence (that's what "convergence" means) — not a bug — but the magnitude is materially larger than the previous proposal and worth game-designer seeing as a number, not just a shape description.

**Q2 — does Menacing Attack stop being worth taking for PureProwess:** DC comparison at identical chassis: **Menacing Attack lands 3-4 points below Trip/Pushing/Disarming Attack's DC at every level (L3 -3, L5 -3, L7 -4, L10 -4), and the gap widens with level, not shrinks.** Same Resolve cost, same maneuver-die bonus damage, same on-hit-required trigger — the only difference is condition type and DC. Verdict: **not strictly dominated in effect-value** (frighten imposes disadvantage on the target's own attacks, a distinct value from Trip's prone/Push's reposition, especially solo with no allies to benefit from prone's granted-advantage), but **in practical land-rate terms it is the clearly worst of the four maneuvers for this archetype from L7 on**, and a rational pure-Prowess Exemplar player will learn to prefer Trip/Push almost every time once the DC gap is visible in play. Call it a real, non-degenerate trade-off at low level that tips into "usually not worth it" at L7+ for this specific build.

**Q3 — win-rate/uptime, 500 trials/cell (16,000 trials total, 4 levels x 2 builds x 2 formulas x 2 monsters):** Overall encounter **win-rate barely moves** (0.0 to -1.6pts in non-boss fights) — these fights were already 90-100% winnable on raw damage alone regardless of CC, so the DC cut doesn't flip outcomes. The real cost lands on **frightened uptime**, which drops **-5.7 to -15.6 points** for PureProwess at L5+ (worst at L7 vs veteran: -13.7pts; L10 vs voidTitan: -15.6pts) while Balanced barely moves (-0.1 to -4.7pts). Boss matchup (voidTitan, CR10, solo L10 PC) win rate is ~1-2% under every formula tested — a pre-existing "unwinnable solo" characteristic of that matchup, not something this formula change causes or fixes.

**Q4 — formula-shape bug, confirmed and quantified:** The plan's own "Engine rule: fractional attribute rounding" section (`docs/plans/2026-07-30-attribute-system-remap.md:101-116`) explicitly documents that summing two SEPARATELY-FLOORED terms (`floor(A/2) + floor(B/2)`) loses a point relative to summing raw then flooring once, and says this exact bug was found and fixed for the concentration formula (line 163) and reused for flee (line 180) — both of which are actually `floor((raw_A + raw_B)/2)`, sum-once, despite the shorthand label on line 163 reading like two separate floors. **The formula given for this review — literally two independent `floor(mod/2)` terms — is written in the shape the plan's own document calls wrong elsewhere in the same file.** Quantified: the as-specified (double-floor) formula underperforms the engine-rule-compliant (sum-raw-floor-once) version by **exactly 1 DC point in 5 of the 8 build×level cells tested** (L3 both builds, L5 Balanced, L7 PureProwess, L10 PureProwess) — occurs whenever `Prowess_mod` and `Presence_mod` are both odd integers (either sign), a real, common occurrence given normal ASI progression, not a rare edge case. **Recommend to `backend-dev`: implement as `8 + prof + floor((Prowess_mod + Presence_mod) / 2)`, not two separate floors** — this is a free, zero-cost fix (no design trade-off, purely arithmetic) that also brings the formula into actual shape-parity with concentration, which is what the request's framing ("matching the already-locked concentration formula shape") asked for in the first place. Even after this fix, PureProwess still loses -2/-3/-3/-3 DC vs today at L3/5/7/10 respectively (recovers ~1 point, doesn't erase the structural convergence cost from Q1) — the fix corrects an arithmetic bug, it does not change the fundamental balance trade-off.

**Verdict:** Not game-breaking. Safe to ship as a genuine design trade-off IF two things happen first: (1) `backend-dev` fixes the double-floor formula shape to sum-raw-floor-once per the plan's own locked precedent (free +1 DC in ~5/8 real cases, no balance cost), and (2) `game-designer` explicitly signs off that a pure-Prowess Exemplar losing 2-4 DC and effectively "outgrowing" Menacing Attack in favor of Trip/Push by L7+ is the intended shape of convergence (it plausibly is — that's what forcing a build-width trade-off means — but it's a bigger bite than the rejected kicker took, worth a conscious yes rather than a default).

Harness: `tools/balance-sim/menacing-attack-convergence-sim.js` (500 trials/cell, same NaN-safe monster list and every-level-ASI PC builds as the two prior passes; computes both the as-specified double-floor formula and the engine-rule sum-once alternative side by side for direct comparison).
