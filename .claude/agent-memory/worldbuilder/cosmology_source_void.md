Established 2026-08-15. Authoritative doc: `docs/world/COSMOLOGY.md`.

## The two forces

- **The Source** — *everything is everything; it's all just the cosmos.* The single continuous cosmos with no edges in it: no boundary between here and there, knower and known, a person and everything they are not. **Separateness is the local condition ordinary existence runs on.** Nothing is powered by it, granted by it, or drawn from it.
- **The Void** — not the removal of the Source but a construct in which the Source was never a term. Not ignorance, not death, not blindness. Absence there is the original condition, not a later one.
- **The symmetry (the centre of the cosmology):** both annihilate the individual, oppositely. Void = you never existed at all. Source = you were never *separate*, not erased but never bounded. Neither is a mercy. **The Source is not the safe one.**
- **Neither is good.** The objection to the Unmooring is entirely ours — that is the setting's moral centre.

## The rejected reading — do not regress to it

A parallel creative-director pass framed the Source as **flatness**: everything at once weighted equally, sensory overload, no hierarchy. **The Chief Designer rejected this.** It is monism, not democracy. Flatness = many things none prioritised, attention fails, overload. Correct = no separate things at all, *boundaries* fail, continuity. My own first draft used a state-resolution ("the Source holds things in a settled state") metaphor and was also wrong — corrected mid-task.

Why the correct reading is right: the rest falls out for free. Transport = there is no distance, separation was never real. Knowledge = no gap between knower and known. Omniscience/omnipresence/omnipotence = one property described by three witnesses who each kept the separateness assumption. Gods = beings who let separateness go further than anything else, so the Departure means they stopped being individuated rather than travelled.

## Knowledge tiers (the structural rule)

Tier A author-truth / Tier B cultural belief / Tier C player-discoverable, kept strictly separate. **The game never confirms the cosmology** — no NPC is right, no book authoritative, no quest ends with an explanation. Content that resolves the Source or the Void is out of canon however well written.

## Terms retired 2026-08-15 (Chief Designer call)

| Old | New | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Wayfinding / wayfinder | **Threading** / threader; a route is *a thread* | From the Verathi "deep weaving" lexicon. Trade vocabulary, no theory behind it. |
| null-flux | **Unheld** | Vethri coinage, mass noun, no article ("Unheld doesn't keep"). *Held* = held apart, kept a distinct thing; Unheld = what's left where that stops. |

Both old coinages were early-draft and never reached player-facing text. Phenomena unchanged; only vocabulary. `data/skillChallenges.json`'s `desert_wayfinding` is unrelated mundane navigation — rename opportunistically.

**Rejected coinage:** "the Half" for null-flux — collides with SRD "Half Plate" in `items.json`/`magicItems.json`.

## Two rules that protect shipped content

- **Void scale rule:** encounter-scale Void reads as *reduction* (things stop working as intended); only regional-scale reads as *never-was*. Never write a Voidborn attack as retroactive unmaking. This keeps the shipped `monsters.json` Void block valid.
- **Voidborn inland** is canon: within living memory they appear far from any mapped boundary. Retro-justifies Voidborn on world-wide encounter tables.

## Traps

- **Serenity is the most dangerous trap.** Union with everything reads as a blessing in most fiction; here it is the loss of every boundary that lets a person be a person. If a passage makes the reader want it, it is wrong. Banned alongside the divine list: transcendence, enlightenment, oneness, harmony, at peace, ascension, awakening, becoming whole.
- **Nobody in the Verge is fluent about this.** Every character reaching for it should sound slightly wrong in their own mouth — borrowed word, self-correction, or stopping. Never write a fluent speaker of the subject.
- Writing the Void with verbs that take a target (devoured, claimed, erased, taken). Loss of a region is not a moment; it is an answer becoming true.
- **Never "the Weave"** — Forgotten Realms IP. Weaving imagery stops at *Aevorn*, *Aevorel*, *weftglass*, *threading*.
- **No "Sourceborn."** The Void has a boundary because it is a neighbouring construct; the Source is not next to anything. It has no creatures, envoys, or faction. Do not invent a symmetric monster line.

**Why:** Chief Designer direction, 2026-08-15 — the two forces are half of what makes Aevorn what it is, alongside its being a convergence point.

**How to apply:** Any Void, Source, godsign, threading, riftborn, or boundary text checks against `docs/world/COSMOLOGY.md` first. Cultures must hold *incompatible partial* readings — never let two agree more than the Cultural Takes section does.
