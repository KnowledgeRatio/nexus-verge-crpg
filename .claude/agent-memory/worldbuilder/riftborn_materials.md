Established 2026-05-25. These are the canonical riftborn materials for Nexus Verge. When writing item descriptions, image prompts, or lore text involving riftborn materials, use these as the reference.

## What riftborn materials are

Composite substances formed at dimensional convergence points over millennia. They cannot exist in single-dimension worlds. They are the most valuable physical things in the Verge. Their defining visual trait: two incompatible material properties coexisting in the same object, observable as a precise physical fact — not as magical glow or atmospheric distortion.

## The six canonical materials

**Vethsteel** — Iron with density banding perpendicular to its hammer-worked grain. The cross-section reveals dark parallel striations like wood rings running through solid iron. Objects made from it sit slightly lower than expected on surfaces. Visually: normal forged iron surface, but internal structure visible in cross-section views.

**Meridian Amber** — Translucent rigid solid whose internal inclusions drift against gravity over hours. Warm dark amber at core, transitioning to pale grey-green at the surface — two different optical densities in one material, boundary that shifts. The edges hold sharp engraving. The inside moves.

**Splitstone** — Grey-white stone that shows two distinct fracture geometries on the same broken face: quartz crystalline cleavage on one half, obsidian conchoidal break on the other. Boundary runs as a razor-straight line across the fractured surface. No visible seam on unbroken surface.

**Thrennwood** — Timber with oak surface grain and finish, but cross-section rings alternating between three distinct wood species from three different climates. No boundary gaps between the rings. One face perpetually slightly damp; the opposite face perpetually slightly dry.

**Coldcast Bronze** — Bronze that maintains two different temperatures simultaneously on opposite faces, with a visible boundary as a faint colour shift: normal oxidised patina on the warm face, persistent pale frost bloom on the cold face. The thermal boundary does not move when the object is held.

**Weftglass** — Dark grey-green glass that casts a shadow of a different shape than its silhouette. Additional planes and edges in the shadow have no corresponding surface on the object. Refractive index shifts by region — different parts of the glass shift the viewed scene in different directions. Consistent and repeatable.

## Rendering rule (for image prompts)

Render the material impossibility as a precise physical fact — a density banding pattern, a frost boundary, a shadow with extra edges. Not as glow, distortion, or aura. The wrongness should be observable, not atmospheric.

**Why:** Defined 2026-05-25 per worldbuilder task. These are the canonical visual identities for the Verge's primary value currency. Consistent rendering is essential for player recognition.

**How to apply:** Reference these when writing item descriptions for riftborn-material items, generating image prompts for such items, or writing lore text about the Verge economy. Style guide at `tools/image-gen/style-guide.md` Item Icons block now references these material concepts.
