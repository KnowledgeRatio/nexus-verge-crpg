Established 2026-05-24 via style-guide.md rewrite.

**Overall palette:** Desaturated earth tones (ash grey, iron brown, deep slate). Isolated warm pops of amber/ember light as accent. NOT bright saturated fantasy.

**Mood:** Weathered and weary — grim-hopeful, not grimdark. Equipment is worn, people look tired.

**Lighting rule:** Single warm source upper-left, cool fill from opposite. Consistent across all asset types.

**Biome accent colours (terrain tiles):**
- Marshes: sickly yellow-green
- Forests: dark teal shadow
- Mountains: blue-grey frost
- Plains: pale straw

**Monster portraits:** Humanoids desperate/hungry (not monstrous-for-fun). Undead: grey/desiccated, cold blue shadows. Beasts: naturalistic but wrong proportions.

**Item rarity colour temperature:**
- Common: raw iron and worn leather
- Uncommon: faint blue-silver sheen
- Rare: cold violet edge-light
- Legendary: deep amber aura with visible heat distortion

**Race portrait backgrounds:**
- Humans: stone towns
- Elves: overgrown remnants
- Dwarves: carved rock interiors
- Halflings: low timber buildings
- Dragonborn: ash-scorched open terrain

**Portrait expression rule:** Serious, watchful — never smiling.

**Why:** The Fracture has made this world hard. Visual tone must reinforce that civilisation is clinging on, magic is unsettling, and nature has gone wrong. Bright heroic fantasy would undermine the setting.

**How to apply:** All image generation prompts, any future visual description in flavour text, and any art-direction notes to other agents or tools should conform to this palette and mood.
