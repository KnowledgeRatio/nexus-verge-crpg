# Nexus Verge — Creatures

## The Voidborn

### What They Are

Entities originating from the Void. Not organisms. Not undead. Not corrupted living things. They are concentrations of void-state that propagate toward complexity — toward life, thought, and structure — because ordered matter is where entropy advances fastest.

They are not alive. They have never been alive. They have no intent, no hunger, no awareness of you. They are physics. The void-state does what entropy does: it moves from order to disorder, and it moves faster in the presence of things worth losing.

This is the horror of them. They don't know you exist. They cannot be reasoned with, bargained with, or turned. They cannot be killed in the conventional sense — you disrupt a concentration, scatter it, seal the rift it propagates through. A defeated Voidborn is not dead. It is temporarily dispersed.

### Writing Rule

**Never use emotional language for Voidborn.** No rage. No hunger. No malice. No intent.

- **Wrong:** "The Void Spawn lunges hungrily, its maw open wide."
- **Right:** "The Void Spawn moves toward the warmth."

- **Wrong:** "The Rift Guardian snarls with hatred."
- **Right:** "The Rift Guardian interposes itself between you and the rift. It does not acknowledge you. It is simply what is in the way."

The emotional register for Voidborn is *weather* and *physics*. A Void Spawn no more hates you than a falling rock does.

### Voidborn Tiers

| Name | CR | Nature | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Void Spawn** | ½ | Small concentration. Phase in and out of material space. | Most common. Appear at rift edges. Touch drains vitality — not painfully, just... reducing. |
| **Void Hunter** | 4 | Larger propagation. Moves faster than physics should allow. | Called "hunters" by the Kethara. They don't hunt — they simply advance toward the highest concentration of ordered matter nearby. |
| **Void Corruptor** | 6 | Spreads void-state into terrain. Corrupts land around itself passively. | Rare. Where one lingers, the land changes. Plants wither without dying. Water stops reflecting. |
| **Void Titan** | 10 | Massive concentration. Seismic. | Rare. Appear only near major rifts. Their movement alone distorts nearby terrain. They do not perceive individual creatures — only the aggregate warmth of a settlement. |

**Boss-tier:** Rift Guardians, Hive Queens, the Overmind — these are major concentrations anchored to specific rift points. They are events, not enemies. The objective is never to "defeat" them in a narrative sense — it is to disrupt or seal the rift that sustains them.

### Voidborn in Dialogue

NPCs describe Voidborn differently depending on culture:

- **Verathi:** By their function. "Convergence accelerants." "The edge made visible." They don't name them emotionally — they name them accurately.
- **Kethara:** By how they die. "The ones that come back." "The pale things." Named for what they do to people, not what they are.
- **Vethri:** Almost no words at all. "They're close." "The pressure's changing." They feel void-state before they see it.
- **Delhari:** Over-categorise. "Class III void-state concentrations." "Entropy propagators." Miss the point that the taxonomy doesn't help you survive them.
- **Sirathi:** By where they came from. "What took Moru." "What's left after." They don't name the Voidborn — they name what the Voidborn took.

## Other Creatures of the Verge

Nexus Verge contains creatures from countless dimensions that have folded into it over time. Most of the game's monster roster represents this — creatures that originated somewhere else and are now part of the Verge's ecology.

**Writing convention:** Creatures that predate the current Void threat are part of the landscape. They are not "corrupted" or "darkened" — they simply exist here the way creatures exist anywhere. A goblin in Nexus Verge is still a goblin. It arrived with some fragment of another world and established itself. It doesn't know about the Void. It's worried about its territory and its food, like goblins always have been.

**Riftborn animals:** Some creatures born in Nexus Verge carry composite properties from the convergence. Larger than expected. Unexpected resistances. Coloring that doesn't match any single origin biome. These are not monsters — they are local fauna adapted to a genuinely strange environment.

**Void-adjacent corruption:** Creatures that live near the advancing Void boundary show behavioural changes — not physical mutation. They are quieter. They don't establish territory. They move in directions that don't make ecological sense. Vethri call this "calibration drift" — the creatures are adjusting to the new reality faster than people are.

## Creature Voice Guidelines

When writing monster flavour text:

1. **What you see** — physical description. Specific. No vague superlatives.
2. **What it does** — one behaviour detail that reveals something true about the world it came from (or the world that made it).
3. **What it means** — one sentence about its place in the ecology or the stakes of encountering it.

**Example (Void Spawn):**
> A pale concentration of void-state barely holding form. It moves toward you the way fog moves toward heat — not attacking, not aware. Just advancing. Whatever it touches stops working as intended.

**Example (standard creature — Hill Troll):**
> Larger than the lowland variant, with hide that catches dimensional residue like moss. It has been here long enough to matter. The terrain around its range is subtly wrong — boulders that shouldn't have rolled where they are, trees stripped in spirals.
