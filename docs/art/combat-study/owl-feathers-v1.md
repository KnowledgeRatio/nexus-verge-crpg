## Final prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless square albedo texture for a
3D owlbear head and neck in an adult fantasy CRPG. Create a flat, orthographic,
edge-to-edge tile of dense small brown-grey owl contour feathers. Fine layered
feather barbs, subtle dark mottled barring, ash grey with muted warm umber,
naturally irregular feather overlaps. Approximately 18 small feather lengths
across the tile. Uniform neutral diffuse lighting, no directional shadows or
baked highlights, no depth of field. This is a material texture, not a portrait:
no head, eyes, beak, animal silhouette, border, words or background. Similar
detail density everywhere, suitable to repeat across a curved model surface.
Real natural plumage, restrained earthy colours; avoid stylized scales, plastic,
metal, cartoon patterns or oversized isolated feathers.

## Use

The image supplies colour detail for the head, facial discs and feather edge
geometry. Existing 3D lighting supplies the overall shading. It is not a normal
map, displacement map or proof of exact seamlessness under every UV layout.
