# Quest tracking and received information

Date: 2026-10-18. Status: tracking slice of [#39](https://github.com/KnowledgeRatio/nexus-verge-crpg/issues/39) implemented and validated locally. Reputation is outside this slice; no issue mutation, closure, commit or deployment.

## Player flow

Track one active quest from the journal; Untrack clears it. Its next required step appears in overworld/dungeon HUD and world-map guidance. Once required fieldwork is complete, guidance returns to the giver without forcing optional investigation. Completion, failure, expiry and abandonment clear the selection.

Guidance uses information actually received. A displayed high-quality acceptance briefing permits an exact entrance; medium permits a globally aligned 64×64 search area without a pin; low gives the saved eight-way direction from the issuer, not a moving player bearing. None or old quests without a receipt stay unknown unless actual discovery proves the location. Board and journal cards do not disclose exact target distances.

Finding a bound site records a durable discovery receipt and upgrades guidance to exact. This includes actual visibility and successful location-reveal consequences. The map and guidance getter do not grant knowledge by being opened.

World-map panning supports mouse, touch and pen through captured pointer events. Secondary pointers/right clicks are ignored; cancellation or lost capture ends the drag. Wheel zoom retains its existing behavior.

## Data and integration

`QuestTracking.js` projects the next available required objective and checks prerequisites. Nonexact views omit hidden target coordinates; medium bounds have exclusive upper limits. `RULES.quests.tracking` owns enablement, area size, mode mapping and bearings.

QuestManager owns selection, acceptance briefings, received-information receipts and discovery refresh. `trackingIntel` records quality, issuer origin, bound target key and low-quality bearing. `trackingDiscoveries['x,y']=true` records known sites. GameState and SaveManager preserve one `trackedQuestId`, default old saves to null and sanitize stale IDs. Generator templates bind briefing text before publication, while only acceptance records received information.

World-map rendering skips compressed saved regions that have no runtime tile array, while still drawing tracking guidance without regenerating regions. This avoids a map crash after loading; it does not repair the separate flat-versus-two-dimensional fog serialization limitation or claim full saved-fog restoration.

## Validation

The final suite passes **1,664 tests in 147 files**, using `npm test -- --maxWorkers=2 --testTimeout=60000` (65.66 seconds). An earlier run during concurrent checks hit two existing expensive combat-test timeouts at 30 seconds; no production/test changes separated that run from the isolated pass. Final web build passes with 184 app files and 107 media files. Repository lint retains 175 existing errors and reports 1,131 warnings; broader lint retains 3,251 errors/2,376 warnings. The new tracking helper and system/UI regression files are clean.

The [desktop/mobile browser artifact](../../tools/balance-sim/quest-tracking-browser.results.json) covers 1440×900 and 390×844. It uses a real composed offer on controlled town/site metadata, actual acceptance and Track/Untrack buttons, a world-map search area and a SaveManager JSON roundtrip. It checks fixed low-quality bearing after player movement, unknown guidance without a receipt, exact guidance after injected canonical discovery, return-to-giver after injected fieldwork and clearing on abandonment. No regions are generated by map inspection, source feature types stay unchanged and no page errors are reported.

The final artifact also verifies real CDP touch panning on mobile and mouse panning on desktop, bringing the search area into view. A SaveManager roundtrip with one real loaded region preserves guidance and leaves the restored region count at one without regeneration or page errors. These are controlled browser states, not a naturally played journey. Tier acceptance and visibility-based discovery also have unit coverage.
