# First Main Campaign — Consultation Brief

This captures the sponsor's campaign consultation through 2026-10-28. The foundations below are sponsor-confirmed direction; the campaign is not approved for implementation. Opening treatments remain alternatives for discussion. This is a design reference, not a roadmap or a claim about current gameplay.

## Sponsor-confirmed foundations

- **An authored campaign within a procedural world.** A connected main questline should carry the narrative alongside recurring and procedural quests, with authored set pieces placed within the generated world. Placement guarantees and implementation remain to be designed.
- **An intentional arrival, an uncertain past.** This campaign's protagonist meant to reach the place they come to know as Aevorn or the Verge, without knowing about the Voidborn threat. Their reasons remain open to player imagination. Gaps concern the lead-up to arrival. The sponsor welcomes the possibility that the backstory the player imagines could prove unreliable; neither the cause nor the extent of false memories is decided.
- **An odyssey from ordinary person to earned leader.** The protagonist begins as another person trying to orient themselves and survive. Through the journey, their actions and relationships can make them a true hero and leader in a collective effort to repel the Voidborn threat, at least temporarily.
- **Competent defenders with competing priorities.** The opening has standing soldiers or an organised militia accustomed to Voidborn incidents. Incidents are becoming more frequent or changing in some way. An unusually bad incident need not dominate public priorities: rivalries, supplies and dangerous creatures also matter. People disagree about the response; humanoids do not automatically sit at the top of the food chain.
- **A practical foothold.** A recurring contact offers help from a mixture of kindness and self-interest. An initial offer of lodging and work can coexist with other jobs and opportunities. This relationship can persist throughout the campaign unless events intervene; no tragedy is mandated. Following up an overdue delivery is the accepted working starting job, with its details still tentative.
- **Participation remains the player's choice.** The main quest invites involvement through useful work, relationships and discoveries. Players can pursue side quests and exploration. Attachment to people or places can motivate participation without imposing a single emotional response or personal home.
- **Travel reveals connections.** Work brings the protagonist into different communities, where apparently local Voidborn incidents gradually suggest a larger pattern. Local knowledge and relationships matter to that discovery.
- **Bringing rivals together requires choices.** Reconciliation is part of the story, but not every alliance can be secured through sufficient effort. Some commitments entail meaningful tradeoffs. The coalition and its consequences should reflect those choices.
- **Occupation and liberation are possibilities.** Settlements becoming occupied and needing liberation may provide campaign events or other adventures, building on applicable game features. Which forces occupy them, when this happens and how campaign integration works remain undecided.

## Direction extending beyond this campaign

Settlements should differ in security arrangements, militarisation, insecurity, peace and prosperity. The opening's defenders do not define every settlement. This is broader worldbuilding direction for later domain review, not a claim that a settlement-security simulation exists. Existing [civics](../world/CIVICS.md), [diplomacy](../world/DIPLOMACY.md) and [creature ecology](../world/CREATURES.md) provide the starting context.

## Canon boundaries

- [World names and the hero](../world/WORLD.md): Aevorn is the Verathi name, “the Verge” common speech, and “Nexus Verge” outsider/cartographic usage. General hero canon allows origins outside or within the Verge; this campaign's newcomer premise does not rewrite that broader freedom.
- [Voidborn](../world/CREATURES.md) and [cosmology](../world/COSMOLOGY.md): the Void and Voidborn have no intent or malice. Temporary dispersal or disruption can support a meaningful victory; a conscious cosmic invader is not established. Occupation must not silently turn the Voidborn into a conventional political army.
- Memory gaps and false-memory possibilities do not establish a Voidborn memory-altering power, a chosen-one identity, or a settled explanation of the setting's mysteries. Relevant history remains governed by [world canon](../world/HISTORY.md).

## Proposals and unresolved questions

The assistant suggested milestone-driven escalation and no silent punishment for exploring; the sponsor has not ruled on that timing model. Accepted missions with urgency, occupation triggers and consequences for postponement need explicit design.

The contact's identity, settlement, delivery contents and fate, first discoveries, memory mystery's cause and resolution, coalition members, and final objective remain open. Neither a defence finale nor an expedition finale has been selected. The earlier “The Road That Remains” outline is not an adopted campaign plot.

## Proposed opening treatments

These worldbuilder/game-designer alternatives are not selected. Contact roles, events and supporting characters are provisional.

### A — The overdue cart

Arrive while an organised militia responds to an unusually bad but familiar Voidborn incident. The protagonist provides bounded help under local direction, without exceptional powers. A carrier offers a bed and paid work tracing an overdue cart: kindness towards a newcomer also protects the carrier's livelihood.

The driver stopped at an unsafe route. Recovering the goods or returning with verified facts offers a way forward. Militia repairs and civilian trade compete for attention and resources. The driver's account echoes something witnessed during arrival, offering an optional lead into the wider pattern.

### B — The held shipment

Arrive in the aftermath, as experienced defenders resume their other duties. An innkeeper offers a bed and paid work tracing a missed delivery. The inn needs goods to feed guests and displaced residents; a neighbouring settlement has held the shipment because danger has worsened and it has needs of its own.

Negotiation or a return with reliable facts can move the job forward. An account from the neighbouring settlement reveals an earlier, similar incident, inviting further inquiry without requiring it.

### Design guidance for discussion

The agents recommend **A** for firsthand participation in the opening emergency; **B** gives negotiation and competing community needs earlier prominence. Either should keep independent side work viable. Skill checks should affect information, costs and options rather than be the sole gate to essential progress. Guaranteeing reachable narrative sites, evidence and a return route within seeded surrounding variation is proposed integration work, not a verified capability. Neither treatment settles the memory mystery's cause or revelation.

## Next consultation

The sponsor can choose, combine or reject these treatments. Develop the first stretch together before fixing the full plot or ending.

No production code, world canon or GitHub backlog changes are authorised by this brief itself. Existing feature support must be checked against code before implementation claims or integration commitments are made.
